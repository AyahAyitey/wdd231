Where to Eat

Start in busy, well-known neighborhoods and use these practical tips to choose a clean vendor.

Popular food areas

Makola

Bustling market lanes

Try: Waakye, kenkey and smoked fish

Go in the morning for the widest breakfast choice.

Osu

Central nightlife and dining area

Try: Kelewele, chichinga and bofrot

Many evening vendors gather near busy intersections.

Jamestown

Historic coastal neighborhood

Try: Fresh fish, kenkey and gari

Pair a market visit with a walk along the waterfront.

Labadi

Beachside food stop

Try: Grilled fish, chichinga and drinks

Ask the vendor to point out today’s freshly grilled fish.

Kaneshie

Major market and transport hub

Try: Red-red, waakye and porridge

Choose stalls with steady customer turnover.

Adabraka

Central residential area

Try: Sobolo, bofrot and Hausa koko

Afternoon and morning are good times for different snacks.

Chorkor

Coastal fishing community

Try: Smoked fish, kenkey and hot sauce

Look for prepared-to-order fish and covered accompaniments.

Simple hygiene checks

  • Check the crowdPopular stalls often have fresh, fast turnover.
  • Watch food handlingLook for covered food and clean utensils.
  • Start gentleAsk for less pepper if spicy food is new to you.
  • Drink safelyChoose sealed bottled water or well-known drinks.
