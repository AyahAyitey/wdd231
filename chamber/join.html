NP Membership Benefits

No Annual Fee

  • Listing in the online member directory
  • Invitations to community outreach events
  • Access to Chamber newsletters and announcements
  • Networking with local businesses and NGOs
  • Use of Chamber meeting rooms (limited)

Bronze Membership Benefits

GH₵ 500 / year

  • All NP Membership benefits
  • Featured listing in the member directory
  • 10% discount on Chamber training workshops
  • Access to member-only networking events
  • Monthly business tips newsletter
  • One free event ticket per quarter

Silver Membership Benefits

GH₵ 1,200 / year

  • All Bronze Membership benefits
  • Spotlight feature on the Chamber home page (rotational)
  • 25% discount on all Chamber events and workshops
  • Priority booth placement at the Annual Business Expo
  • Logo placement in Chamber marketing materials
  • Quarterly one-on-one business advisory session
  • Social media shoutout once per quarter

Gold Membership Benefits

GH₵ 2,500 / year

  • All Silver Membership benefits
  • Permanent spotlight feature on the Chamber home page
  • Free entry to all Chamber events and workshops
  • Premium booth at the Annual Business Expo (corner location)
  • Dedicated feature article in the Chamber quarterly magazine
  • Monthly business advisory sessions with Chamber leadership
  • Speaking opportunity at one Chamber event per year
  • Exclusive invitation to Gold Member roundtable dinners
